About the Laboratory

Built on Rigor and Trust

Chemolab Laboratory Ltd was established with a single purpose: to provide industrial and commercial clients with analytical results they can act on with confidence. Our team of experienced chemists operates within a strict quality management system, calibrated instruments, and a culture of scientific accountability.

  • State-of-the-art instrumentation — maintained and calibrated to international reference standards.
  • Rapid turnaround — standard 3–5 business day reporting, with express 24–48 hour options available.
  • Independent & impartial — results are never influenced by commercial pressure; our integrity is our product.
  • Full documentation — every analysis is accompanied by complete chain-of-custody records and signed certificates.
